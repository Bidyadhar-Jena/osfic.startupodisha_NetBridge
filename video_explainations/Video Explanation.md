# NetBridge -- Video Explanation

## Project Overview

**NetBridge** is a project developed as part of the **Odisha Seva First
Innovation Challenge (OSFIC)**, organised through OSFIC -- Startup
Odisha.

-   **Problem domain:** Information & Communication Technology (ICT)
-   **Project focus:** Exploring offline digital payment possibilities
    and addressing connectivity challenges.
-   **Key considerations:** Accessibility, reliability, transaction
    authentication, and security.

## Project Highlights

-   Exploring the possibilities of offline digital payments.
-   Understanding transaction workflows and authentication mechanisms.
-   Examining the role of CA certificates in digital security.
-   Considering connectivity and security challenges.
-   Exploring approaches to improve digital financial accessibility.
